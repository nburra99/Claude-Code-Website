The process

How a student gets better here

No mystery, no filler sessions. Four steps, in this order.

  1. 01

    Free diagnostic

    A 45-minute session where we find the actual gap — which is usually two years below the topic being struggled with.

  2. 02

    A plan, in writing

    You get a written plan: target topics, session cadence, and what "on track" looks like by when.

  3. 03

    Weekly sessions

    50 minutes, one-to-one or in a group of three. Every session ends with work the student does alone.

  4. 04

    Progress you can see

    A short report after every session, plus a full review at the six-week mark.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the first session really free?

Yes. It's a full 45-minute diagnostic session and you get a written plan afterwards. If it isn't a fit, you owe nothing and we won't chase you.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both. Online sessions use a shared whiteboard and are recorded so the student can rewatch them. In-person sessions run at our study rooms.

How long before grades improve?

Most students see a test-score change within six to eight weeks. Foundational gaps take longer to close properly, and we'll tell you honestly at the start which case you're in.

Can you match a specific syllabus?

Yes — AP, IB, honors and state standards including NY Regents. Send us the syllabus or textbook and we'll map the plan to it.

What if the tutor isn't the right fit?

Switch any time at no cost. The match matters more than the schedule, and we'd rather you move than drift.
